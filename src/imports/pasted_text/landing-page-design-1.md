## 🌿 A suggestion that I think could become PathPilot's signature

Instead of putting statistics like:

* "10,000 students"
* "95% satisfaction"
* "50 universities"

(which you won't have during the competition),

use a **"Student Moments"** section that immediately resonates with your audience.

Imagine three simple cards:

> **"I don't even know where to start."**
>
> PathPilot helps you choose your top three priorities.

---

> **"I have work after class."**
>
> PathPilot adjusts your study plan around your schedule.

---

> **"I forgot what the exam covers."**
>
> Upload your lecture slides and get summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and likely exam topics.

This is powerful because it's not selling features—it's reflecting the thoughts every student has. Judges are much more likely to remember an app that solves familiar problems than one that lists technical capabilities. I think this section perfectly reinforces your message:

> **Because students carry more than deadlines.**
